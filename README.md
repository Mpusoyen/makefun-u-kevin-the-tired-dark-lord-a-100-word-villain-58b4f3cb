A 97-word monologue for Malachar Vane, keeper of the Hollow Throne for three hundred years, who raised the drowned bells of Cassivel and taxed the moon — and whose scheme to toll the Ninth Bell at midnight and walk the sea inland keeps trailing off into candle complaints, lost threads, and long waits for an hour that never arrives. The tiredness is never announced; it lives in the pauses, the deflections, and a last line that lets the bell toll without him.
